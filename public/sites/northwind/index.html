Product details

The Trail Jacket has been our best-selling shell for six seasons running. This year we rebuilt it with a quieter, softer 40D recycled nylon face fabric, a laminated hood visor that stays out of your eyes, and two-way pit zips that open and close without taking your pack off.

  • 3-layer 40D recycled nylon with a PFC-free DWR finish
  • Fully seam-taped; 20,000 mm waterproof and 20,000 g breathable
  • Helmet-compatible hood with a one-pull adjuster and laminated visor
  • Two-way pit zips, two zippered hand pockets and one chest pocket
  • Packs into its own chest pocket and weighs 11.4 oz (323 g) in size M
  • Bluesign® approved fabric, sewn in a Fair Trade Certified™ factory
