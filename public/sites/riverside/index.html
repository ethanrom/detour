Who can apply

You may be eligible for FreshStart Food Assistance if you live in Riverside County and your household's gross monthly income is at or below the limit for your household size. Most people who apply for the first time are surprised to learn they qualify. If you are not sure, apply anyway: it costs nothing and the county will tell you what you are eligible for.

Residency

You must live in Riverside County at the time you apply. You do not need a permanent address. People staying in shelters, motels, vehicles or with friends and family can apply using the address of any county office as their mailing address. You do not have to live here for any minimum length of time.

Your household

A household is everyone who lives together and buys and prepares food together. Spouses and children under 22 who live with you are always counted as part of your household, even if you eat separately. Roommates who buy and cook their own food can apply as a separate household.

Count the income of every person in the household, before taxes and deductions. This includes wages, self-employment income, Social Security, unemployment, child support and pensions. Some income, such as one-time gifts, student loans and most tax refunds, is not counted.

Citizenship and immigration status

U.S. citizens and many lawfully present non-citizens qualify, including refugees, asylees and lawful permanent residents. Children who are citizens can receive benefits even if their parents do not qualify. Household members who are not applying for benefits are not asked about their immigration status.

Students, older adults and people with disabilities

College students enrolled at least half time may qualify if they work 20 hours a week, take part in work-study, care for a young child or receive CalWORKs-style cash aid. Households with a member who is 60 or older, or who has a disability, have a higher resource limit and may deduct certain medical costs from their income.

Work requirements

Most adults aged 18 to 54 without children must work, volunteer or attend training for at least 80 hours a month to receive benefits for more than three months. Exceptions apply if you are pregnant, caring for a child or an incapacitated person, experiencing homelessness, or unable to work for health reasons. Your caseworker will go over the options with you.

What you will need
  • Proof of identity, such as a driver's license, school ID or passport
  • Proof of where you live, such as a lease, utility bill or a letter from a shelter
  • Recent pay stubs or other proof of income for everyone in your household
  • Your most recent rent or mortgage statement and utility bills
  • Social Security numbers for household members who are applying

Missing a document should not stop you from applying. Submit what you have, and your caseworker will help you get the rest.

Households with a member who is 60 or older or has a disability are not subject to the gross income limit, but their net income must still be within program limits.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need to be a U.S. citizen to apply?
No. Many lawfully present non-citizens qualify, and citizen children can receive benefits even when their parents do not. Household members who are not applying are not asked about their immigration status, and applying will not affect their case.
How long does it take to find out if I am approved?
Most applications are decided within 30 days. If your household has less than $150 in monthly income and $100 or less in cash, or your rent and utilities are more than your income and cash, you may qualify for expedited service and receive a decision within 3 days.
Can I apply if I have a job?
Yes. More than half of the households receiving FreshStart benefits include someone who works. What matters is your household's total income compared to the limit for your household size.
How do I use my benefits?
You will receive an EBT card in the mail within 7 days of approval. Use it like a debit card at grocery stores, corner markets and participating farmers markets. Benefits are loaded onto your card on the same day each month, and unused funds roll over.
What if I do not have a permanent address?
You can still apply. Use the address of a shelter, a friend or family member, or any county office as your mailing address. Tell your caseworker you are experiencing homelessness so they can speed up your application.
What happens after I apply?
A caseworker will call you within 10 days to complete a short interview. You will then receive a letter explaining the decision and, if you are approved, how much you will receive each month and how long your benefits last.
Ready to apply?

The online application takes about 20 minutes. You can save your progress and come back later. You can also apply by phone at 1-800-555-0142 or in person at any of our 14 offices.
