Product details

The Summit Jacket is built for the cold, still moments between pitches and on the summit. A 15D recycled ripstop shell keeps the down dry in light weather, and baffles are sewn through so there are no cold spots.

  • 800-fill-power recycled down, traceable to the source
  • 15D recycled ripstop with a PFC-free water-repellent finish
  • Stretch-bound hood, cuffs and hem seal in warmth
  • Two zippered hand pockets and one chest pocket
  • Packs into its own pocket; 9.8 oz (278 g) in size M
